When the Candle Tilted

Lilpoet Ezra

When the candle tilted, the room did not panic. It only shifted its shadows across the wall like people leaving without wanting to be heard. Outside, Nairobi kept moving: a boda passing, a gate closing, rain considering the roof then choosing restraint. Inside, your name stayed where I had left it, near the matchbox, near the cup gone cold, near the sentence I had no courage to finish. The flame leaned. For a moment, everything became honest: the table, the dust, the small ache that had been sitting with me all evening without asking for space. I steadied the candle. I did not steady myself. Some light survives by bending first.
